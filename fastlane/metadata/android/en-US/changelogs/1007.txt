Stay reachable: an option to keep the connection to your exchange open from
the phone itself, so calls and messages arrive while Sigil is not in front
and without installing a push distributor. It is off until you turn it on,
and says so in the notification shade while it runs.

A ring that reached a phone with the app in the background used to arrive
silently. It announces itself now.

Your devices can be answered from the shade as well as from the card.
