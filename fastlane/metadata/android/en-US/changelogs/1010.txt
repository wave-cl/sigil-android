Video clips: a bubble draws the clip's own still while it is still going
up, and a tap opens it on the whole screen -- pictures too -- with a name
to save it under.

The backup keeps itself up to date (SIP-48), and says so where the dates
are.

A channel's settings are rows that say what they do, with what cannot be
undone kept behind a button of its own. The directory arrives full rather
than filling in. Rows are sized for a finger.
